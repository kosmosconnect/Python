Class 9 · Lists

A list keeps items in order

lists.py

marks = [78, 92, 65, 88]
print(marks[0], marks[-1])
print(marks[1:3])
print(len(marks))
marks[2] = 70
print(marks)

Output

78 88
[92, 65]
4
[78, 92, 70, 88]
