Working with dates

dates.py

from datetime import date
today = date.today()
print("Today:", today)
exam = date(2026, 12, 15)
left = (exam - today).days
print("Days to exam:", left)
print(today.strftime("%d/%m/%Y"))

Output

Today: 2026-10-06
Days to exam: 70
06/10/2026
