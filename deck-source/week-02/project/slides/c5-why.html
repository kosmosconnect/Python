Class 5 · Why loops

Print 1 to 100: two ways

Without a loop

print(1)
print(2)
print(3)
print(4)
# ...and 96 more lines!

With a loop

for i in range(1, 101):
    print(i)

Two lines instead of 100. Change 101 to 1001 and it is still two lines.

while

Repeat as long as a condition is True. Use it when you don't know how many rounds.

for

Repeat for each number or item. Use it when you know how many rounds.

Python Programming · Week 2 · Class 5

Analogy: a gym trainer says "do 20 push-ups", not "push-up, push-up, push-up..." twenty times. A loop is that single instruction. Each repetition is called an iteration. Examples to sort into while vs for: "keep asking for the password until it is correct" (while: unknown rounds); "print the 7 times table" (for: exactly 10 rounds); "keep reading digits until the number becomes 0" (while).
